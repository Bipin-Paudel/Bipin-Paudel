## 🔬 Flagship Research: ResearchGap AI

> **An AI-powered literature review pipeline that uses NLP, semantic embeddings, and clustering to mathematically quantify and identify research gaps in scientific papers.**

Finding genuine research gaps usually requires manually reading hundreds of papers. **ResearchGap AI** transitions this into an automated, mathematically grounded framework:
- Ingests academic literature and generates high-dimensional semantic embeddings using **Sentence-BERT**.
- Applies **K-Means clustering** to partition research themes and identify clusters.
- Performs bibliometric analysis (publication trends, recency, and citation density) to pinpoint sparse, underexplored areas.
- Provides a clean **Next.js** dashboard for collecting papers, exploring clusters, and exporting structured research gap reports.
